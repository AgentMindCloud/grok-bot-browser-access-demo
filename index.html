Can I use Grok Bot from a browser without installing the app?

No. There is no Grok Bot browser client today. grok.com is Grok chat, a different product, and it does not list your bots.

Grok Bot is included with Cursor Pro, Pro+, Ultra, and self-serve Teams, and with a linked SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, or SuperGrok Heavy subscription. A Cursor plan already covers it. Buying SuperGrok is not required.

On a computer where you cannot install the app

Use the Grok Bot app on your phone and sign in with the same Cursor account. That does not install anything on this computer. The phone app opens the same bots, conversations, routines, and cloud computer.

  • iPhone and iPad: App Store
  • Android: Google Play
  • Setup: Grok Bot on mobile and Grok Bot for Mobile

Updating, recovering, or resetting the bot computer is still desktop-only. Do that on a machine where the desktop app is already allowed. Messaging your bots from the phone does not require it.
